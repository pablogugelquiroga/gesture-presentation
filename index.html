Gesture Controlled

Navigate with your hand and eyes. Move the mouse to aim, hold left-click to curl. Close eyes = both eyes shut.

How it works

  • Move mouse → index finger cursor
  • Hold click (0.4s) → curl → next slide
  • Hold click twice quickly → double curl
  • Press B key → both eyes closed → prev slide

Powered by MediaPipe

21 hand landmarks and 468 face mesh points tracked in real-time at 30fps, all running locally in your browser.
